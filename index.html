This is your factory.

One idea was one line. Every idea gets its own line, all running at once, on the computers you choose.

How it works

  1. Step 1Paste one line into your agent. The agent you already use sets everything up.
  2. Step 2Answer a few questions. Your agent asks three at a time until the idea is clear.
  3. Step 3Approve the plan. One look at the tickets and how they fit together.
  4. Step 4Watch it ship. Agents build, review and merge, and you see every step.
