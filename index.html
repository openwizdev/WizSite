How it works

  • EngineOpenWiz runs your factory. Builds, reviews and merges approved plans. CLI and web app.
  • SkillsWizSkills are the skills your agents follow. Grill ideas, plan tickets, build and review.
  • PluginsWizPlugins put the controls in Cloudroom. Cloudroom plugins for OpenWiz workflows.

You decide what gets built. WizSkills tell the agents how. OpenWiz runs the line and shows you every step.
