0.0.5

Nov 5, 2026

Fleets of agents build for you

  1. Eight fleet skills for coding agents: grill you on an idea, plan it into tickets, then build, review and merge each one.
  2. They ship as a Claude Code plugin, and as skill folders Codex reads from the repo.
  3. Every merge needs an independent review of the exact commit and green CI.

0.0.4

Nov 5, 2026

Start new threads, with files

  1. openwiz new starts a Cloud thread in one of your projects. It shows exactly what it will do, and only creates it with --yes.
  2. Attach files and images to a new thread or a queued message, from the terminal or the web page.
  3. One thread per create: if the result is unclear, it tells you instead of trying again.
0.0.4 on GitHub ↗

0.0.3

Nov 5, 2026

Message your threads

  1. openwiz send sends one message to a thread, as Queue or Steer.
  2. Or send from the web page on your phone.
  3. If a send's result is unclear, OpenWiz says so instead of sending twice.

0.0.2

Oct 30, 2026

Your threads on your phone

  1. A private web page served from your own computer: threads by project, with status and age; tap one for details.
  2. Reach it from anywhere through a Cloudflare Tunnel and a login for your email only.
  3. Read-only, and it tells you when data is stale or you're signed out.

0.0.1

Oct 30, 2026

Your CloudRoom threads in the terminal

  1. openwiz ls lists your threads with status, project and age; -l adds a recap, model and effort.
  2. openwiz show <id> shows one thread and its final output.
  3. --json for scripts. Read-only: it never changes a thread.
