Market

Drivers, harnesses, skills and anything else that plugs into OpenWiz, listed by anyone in WizMarket. Verified means it passed its kind's test: a driver or harness by its kit receipt from its real host, a skill by its own check at a pinned commit. Listed is a link, not proven.

Publish your own

Anyone with a GitHub account can list what they built: add one file, listings/<id>.json, to WizMarket by pull request, and tag it with what it is, in your own words. The listing format is one page: the fields, what proves a verified listing, and what stays out. Once it merges, it appears here at the site's next refresh of the registry: verified if its proof passed WizMarket's check, listed otherwise.
