How the call works

  • A small, real change of your choice.
  • On a repository with working CI and a signed-in coding agent (Claude Code or Codex).
  • If it hasn't shipped in 30 minutes, Jake stays on until it does.

The 30 minutes, roughly

  1. Minute 0

    We connect your repo and your agents.

  2. Minute 10

    You answer a few questions about your change.

  3. Minute 20

    Your agents build it.

  4. Minute 30

    A pull request ships.

Ships means a pull request opens on your repo, built by your agents and passing your CI. Merging is your call.
